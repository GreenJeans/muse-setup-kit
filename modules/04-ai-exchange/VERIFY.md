# Verify — Module 04: AI Exchange

## At install

- [ ] The folder tree exists: protocol doc, queue sheet, per-agent
      Inbox/Outbox, Published folder.
- [ ] Sharing audit at the source: the tree sits outside any
      household-shared folder; every piece's sharing matches the
      protocol (participants reach their own inbox/outbox and Published,
      nothing else).
- [ ] The queue sheet has all columns: ID, Created, Task, Requester,
      Owner agent, Status, Input link, Output link, Provenance/sources,
      Ingested, Ingested into, Rejected/disputed.
- [ ] Each participant's onboarding note states the output contract.

## Deferred

- [ ] **Canary round-trip** (due: setup week): each participant reads
      its inbox and writes the canary to its own outbox — verified at the
      source, in the right folder, by the right agent.
- [ ] **First real task** (due: first month): completes under the output
      contract (DONE / BLOCKED / PARTIAL stated), and its queue row shows
      provenance and where the output was ingested — or why it was
      rejected.
