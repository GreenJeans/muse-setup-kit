# Promises & waiting-on ledger

<!-- Template from the Muse Setup Kit (module 02). One entry per
commitment between the user and another person: what the user
owes, and what the user is waiting on. Every entry carries a
grade — how its words are held:

- verbatim: the commitment in the words as actually said or
  written, in quotes, with a source locator;
- user's own record: the commitment rests on the user's own note
  or receipt — firm as to fact, wording marked unverified, the
  source locator is the user's record;
- unverified: the words cannot be recovered — the paraphrase is
  recorded as a note, never in quotes as the other party's
  words, and the entry is marked unverified.

A paraphrase is never dressed as a quote, and nothing is
reconstructed from memory. Entries name other people and quote
their words (procedures §13, third-party class): this file
lives in a local workspace file, never on a shared surface. -->
